We will use historical stock price data for Netflix to train and test machine learning models. The data consists of the following columns:

Date: The date of the stock price

Open: The opening price of the stock on that day

High: The highest price of the stock on that day

Low: The lowest price of the stock on that day

Close: The closing price of the stock on that day

Adj Close: The adjusted closing price of the stock on that day. ‘Adj Close’ is short for ‘Adjusted Closing Price’. It is the closing price of a stock adjusted for any corporate actions such as stock splits, dividends, and rights offerings that occurred after the market close of the previous trading day.

Volume: The trading volume of the stock on that day. In the context of stock trading, ‘Volume’ refers to the total number of shares of a particular stock that were traded on a given day. It is a measure of the level of activity in the stock market for that stock.

Input and Out columns:

In this case, we want to predict the future stock price of Netflix. So, we will use the ‘Close’ column as our output label or target variable. We will use the other columns, ‘Open’, ‘High’, ‘Low’, ‘Adj Close’ and ‘Volume’ as our input features.

Why Close column as a label?

The ‘Close’ price of a stock is the final price at which it is traded on a given day. It is a commonly used metric to track the performance of a stock. In this project, we are trying to predict the future stock price of Netflix, and the ‘Close’ price is the most relevant and appropriate target variable for our prediction model.

Using ‘Close’ as our output label makes sense because it is the ultimate goal of any investor — to buy a stock at a lower price and sell it later at a higher price. By predicting the future ‘Close’ price of Netflix, we can make informed decisions about when to buy and sell the stock, thus maximizing our profit.

Additionally, the ‘Close’ price is influenced by the ‘Open’, ‘High’, and ‘Low’ prices of the stock. Hence, by including these features in our prediction model, we can get a more accurate estimate of the future ‘Close’ price.
